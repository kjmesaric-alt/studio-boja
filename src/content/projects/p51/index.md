On the upper floor of a culturally protected house in the Slovenian forest, two small guest bedrooms were opened up into one light-filled living room, centred on a fireplace and a wall of glass balcony doors. With the existing living area downstairs lacking natural light, the client wanted an open, inviting space to settle into at the end of the day. To keep room for guests, a single bed is integrated beside the fireplace, doubling as a cosy nook, alongside an extendable modular sofa. Natural materials and vintage furniture pieces bring a rustic warmth to the modern space, giving it a character that suits both quiet evenings and a full house.
